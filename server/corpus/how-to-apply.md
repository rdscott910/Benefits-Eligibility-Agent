# How to apply for NC FNS (Food and Nutrition Services)

Snapshot of the NCDHHS "Apply for Food and Nutrition Services" page and
FNS Manual section 400 (How to Apply,
https://policies.ncdhhs.gov/wp-content/uploads/fns-400-how-to-apply-for-fns-1.pdf).
Food and Nutrition Services (FNS, also called food stamps or SNAP) is
administered in North Carolina by county Departments of Social Services
(DSS).

## Three ways to apply

1. Apply online with ePASS at epass.nc.gov. You can create an account to
   manage your benefits, or apply for FNS without creating an account.
2. Apply in person at your county Department of Social Services (DSS)
   office. You can fill out as much of the paper application as possible
   ahead of time and bring your documents with you. You can also drop
   off a completed application at the DSS office.
3. Apply by mail (or fax). Fill out a paper application (form DSS-8207,
   Application for Food and Nutrition Services) and send it to your
   local county DSS office.

Benefits start from the day the application is submitted, even if it is
incomplete — you can go back and add information after the application
has been submitted.

## Documents to gather

Gather as many of these documents as you can easily get, but do not let
that stop you from submitting the application:

- Identification for you or the head of the household (a driver's
  license, birth certificate, or other government-issued ID).
- Social Security numbers, birthdates, and citizenship status for each
  person in the household.
- Proof of income and expenses for the household.

## The interview

The next step after submitting is an interview with a case worker. They
will talk with you about your eligibility, how much in benefits you can
expect to get, and next steps. If you apply online, a case worker will
contact you within a few days; the interview can take place in person,
by telephone, or through video conference. If you apply in person, you
can be interviewed during your visit or schedule the interview for a
future time. You may choose someone to represent you in the interview.

## Processing times

Per the DSS-8560 Caseworker Desk Reference: households entitled to
expedited (emergency) service must receive benefits no later than the
7th calendar day after applying; normal processing is completed by the
30th calendar day. An application can be reopened if verifications are
provided by the 60th day from the date of application.

## Where decisions are made

Eligibility is determined by income, household size, assets, and many
other factors. Because the calculation is complex, the best way to find
out if you are eligible is to apply. Only the county Department of
Social Services can determine eligibility and benefit amounts.
