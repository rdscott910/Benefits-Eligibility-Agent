# NC FNS household composition — who counts in an FNS unit

Snapshot of NCDHHS FNS Manual section 210 (Household Composition). The
FNS unit — the set of people whose income and circumstances are counted
together — is based on living and meal arrangements, not just family
relationship.

## What counts as an FNS unit

An FNS unit is one of the following:

- A person living alone; or
- A group of people living together who purchase and prepare meals
  together, regardless of relationship to each other; or
- A person or group of people who state they will purchase and prepare
  meals separately from the others with whom they live upon receipt of
  Food and Nutrition Services benefits.

At application, recertification, and any reported change in household
composition, the agency determines the total number of people living in
the residence, their relationships, their ages, and the arrangements for
purchasing and preparing meals.

## Required household members (must be in the same FNS unit)

The following individuals must be included in the same FNS unit:

- Individuals who live together and purchase and prepare their food
  together, regardless of their relationship to each other.
- Spouses living together, regardless of whether they purchase and
  prepare their food together. Spouse refers to individuals who are
  married to each other under applicable state law.

## Separate household members (may be their own FNS unit)

Individuals who are not required or excluded FNS household members may
be a separate FNS unit from other household members if they state they
purchase and prepare food separately from others in the home, or will do
so upon receipt of FNS benefits.

## Separate FNS unit status for certain elderly and disabled persons

Certain elderly and disabled persons may be granted separate FNS unit
status when living with others, if the income of the people with whom
they reside does not exceed the maximum allowable monthly amounts below
(165% maximum allowable gross income limit, effective October 1, 2025,
per FNS Manual section 360.03):

| FNS unit size | 165% maximum allowable gross income limit |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $2,152 |
| 2 | $2,909 |
| 3 | $3,665 |
| 4 | $4,421 |
| 5 | $5,177 |
| 6 | $5,934 |
| 7 | $6,690 |
| 8 | $7,446 |
| Each additional member | +$757 |

## Special living arrangements

Residents of public or private nonprofit shelters for homeless persons
can participate in the program and must be treated as separate
households from the others with whom they reside, subject to mandatory
household combination rules (for example, spouses in the same shelter
cannot be separate households). Residents of shelters for battered
women and children may be considered an individual FNS unit (including
their children) or a group FNS unit, based on whether food is
customarily purchased and prepared separately or in common with the
group (FNS Manual section 212, Household Composition Special
Arrangements).
