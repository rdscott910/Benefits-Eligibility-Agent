# NC FNS resource (asset) limits and categorical eligibility

Snapshot of NCDHHS FNS Manual section 390 (Resources) and section 220
(Categorical Eligibility,
https://policies.ncdhhs.gov/wp-content/uploads/FNS-220-Categorical-Eligibility-10.2024.pdf).
Resources are money and property such as cash, bank accounts, and
certain vehicles or land.

## Resource limits

The maximum allowable resources, including both liquid and non-liquid
assets, of all members of a non-categorically eligible FNS unit shall
not exceed:

| FNS unit type | Resource limit |
| --- | --- |
| Regular FNS household | $3,000 |
| FNS unit including a member age 60 or over, or a Specified Person (disabled) | $4,500 |
| Categorically eligible FNS unit | No resource limit |

## Categorical eligibility — most households pass automatically

Categorically eligible households are not subject to the resource,
gross income, or net income limits. A household is categorically
eligible when all members receive SSI or Work First Family Assistance
(WFFA), or when the unit's gross income is at or below the 200% maximum
allowable gross income limit and the household has received information
about TANF-funded services. Categorically eligible households
automatically pass the resource test and are not required to verify
resources. Non-categorically eligible households are subject to the
resource, gross, and net income limits; non-categorically eligible
households that contain specified individuals (age 60 or older or
disabled) are subject only to the resource and net income limits.

An FNS unit that loses eligibility due to receiving lottery or gambling
winnings of $4,500 or more cannot be considered categorically eligible
if they reapply; that application is processed using the
non-categorically-eligible resource and income requirements.

## Resources that are not counted

Do not count the following resources:

- Resources belonging to Supplemental Security Income (SSI) recipients.
- Resources included in a bankruptcy order (considered inaccessible
  while part of the order).
- Resources belonging to categorically eligible FNS units.
- Resources belonging to an ineligible student.

You may still qualify if you work part-time, make a little money, or
own a home or car — after you apply, case workers determine which
limits apply to you.
