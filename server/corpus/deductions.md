# NC FNS allowable deductions from income

Snapshot of NCDHHS FNS Manual section 340 (Deductions) and the DSS-8560
Caseworker Desk Reference (revised 10-2025,
https://policies.ncdhhs.gov/wp-content/uploads/DSS-8560-Caseworker-Desk-Reference_12.31.2025.pdf).
Deductions are subtracted from a household's income when determining
net countable income. Dollar figures are effective October 1, 2025.

## Standard deduction

Every FNS unit receives a standard deduction based on unit size
(ineligible members and non-household members are not included when
determining the size used for this deduction):

| FNS unit size | Standard deduction |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $209 |
| 2 | $209 |
| 3 | $209 |
| 4 | $223 |
| 5 | $261 |
| 6 or more | $299 |

## Earned income deduction

20% of gross earned income is deducted. Unearned income (such as
unemployment benefits or Social Security) does not receive this
deduction.

## Dependent care deduction

The actual incurred expense for the care of a child or other dependent
when the care is needed for a household member to work, look for work,
or attend training or school. There is no cap on the dependent care
deduction.

## Excess shelter deduction

Shelter costs (rent or mortgage, property taxes, insurance, and utility
allowances) that exceed half of the household's income after other
deductions may be deducted, up to a cap of $744 per month for FNS units
without a Specified Person (a member age 60 or older or disabled).
There is no cap on the excess shelter deduction for FNS units that
include a Specified Person.

## Homeless shelter deduction

Households in which all members are homeless and that incur or expect
to incur shelter costs may receive a homeless shelter deduction of up
to $198.99 per month. If the homeless shelter deduction is not allowed,
a utility allowance may be allowed if applicable.

## Utility allowances

Standard utility allowances are used in place of actual utility bills,
by FNS unit size (effective October 1, 2025; non-household members are
not included when determining the size used for the SUA or BUA):

| FNS unit size | Standard Utility Allowance (SUA, with heating/cooling) | Basic Utility Allowance (BUA, no heating/cooling) | Telephone Utility Allowance (TUA) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | $637 | $392 | $42 |
| 2 | $699 | $431 | $42 |
| 3 | $768 | $474 | $42 |
| 4 | $837 | $518 | $42 |
| 5 or more | $912 | $564 | $42 |

## Medical deduction for elderly or disabled members

A specified person (a member age 60 or older or disabled) is allowed a
medical deduction for medical expenses that will not be paid or
reimbursed by another source (such as insurance, Medicaid, or
Medicare). Only amounts that exceed $35 per month are deducted; no
medical deduction is given unless the specified person(s) in the FNS
unit have more than $35 in monthly allowable medical expenses.
Allowable expenses include medical and dental care, prescriptions,
health insurance premiums (including Medicare Part D), prescribed
equipment and supplies, and reasonable transportation and lodging for
care. Costs of special diets are not deductible.
